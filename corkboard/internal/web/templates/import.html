What you can upload

Nmap or masscan XML from -oX or -oA. Hosts, services, and hostnames are merged into the inventory.

NetExec protocol DBs such as smb.db or ldap.db from ~/.nxc/workspaces/<workspace>/. Keep the original file name; it identifies the protocol. Credentials and access are imported.

Re-uploading the same file is a no-op. Out-of-scope hosts are flagged and raise the scope alarm.
